# Risk gates

## Hard fail

Reject the idea when evidence or creative direction includes:

- named brands, franchises, characters, celebrities, sports teams, or copied artwork;
- a direct medical cure/treatment/prevention claim;
- unclear buyer, missing product fit, or an incomplete creative mechanic;
- no buyer/marketplace evidence outside a non-social trend claim.

## Review

Keep as `hypothesis`; never auto-shortlist:

- suspicious slogan or phrase needing a trademark search;
- grief/memorial, politics, religion, protected attributes, or children;
- one social source only;
- capped or lower-bound competition where the decision needs an exact threshold.

## Research-qualified

Require all gates to pass, explicit buyer and product, marketplace/purchase evidence, and at least one independent evidence source. Meta ad longevity and social engagement do not satisfy purchase intent.

Scores are a 0–5 vector with named rubrics. Do not collapse the vector into a hidden weighted score.
