# POD idea framework

## Cluster facets

Keep these facets explicit: audience/identity, buyer, wearer or user, occasion, emotion/humor, style/aesthetic, product, and personalization.

Related keywords do not automatically share one buying intent. Keep broad identity, humor, gift/occasion, memorial, and personalization directions separate unless every facet that changes the purchase decision matches.

## Idea mechanics

Use a distinct concept mechanic, not word swaps:

- `text-led`: an original phrase structure and typography system.
- `illustration-led`: a visual metaphor or compositional device.
- `personalization`: customer-supplied name, year, role, or relationship.
- `gift-occasion`: buyer-to-recipient moment and presentation.
- `identity-insider`: language recognizable to a real community.

Every idea card states buyer, wearer/user, occasion, emotion, product types, creative brief, supporting keyword IDs, source types, caveats, and claim levels.

Memorial/grief, medical, political, religious, protected-attribute, and children-related concepts always require sensitive-content review. Named brands, characters, teams, celebrities, copied artwork, and suspicious slogans always require IP review.
