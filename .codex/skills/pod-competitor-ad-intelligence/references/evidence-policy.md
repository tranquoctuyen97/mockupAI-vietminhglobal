# Evidence Policy

- Label visible ad copy, CTA, destination URL, price, offer, screenshots and capture time as observed.
- Label days observed, landing-domain match, creative family grouping and price or offer deltas as derived.
- Label likely angle, funnel role, creative testing or seasonal strategy as inference with caveats.
- Never state spend, targeting, bid, attribution, ROAS, revenue, margin or profit as fact.
- A missing ad in one run is not inactive unless the platform marks it inactive or the documented absence policy is satisfied.
- Do not copy competitor artwork or protected copy into new listings; extract reusable principles only.
